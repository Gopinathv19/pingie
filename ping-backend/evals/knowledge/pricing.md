# Pricing

## Free plan

The Free plan costs $0 and includes 1 bot and 100 messages per month.

## Pro plan

The Pro plan costs $29 per month. It includes 10 seats, 5 bots and priority email support.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise pricing is custom. Contact sales at sales@acme.example for a quote. Enterprise adds single sign-on (SSO) and a dedicated account manager.

## Annual billing

Paying yearly gives two months free on any paid plan.
